<p>Matixray is a fast, lightweight proxy client for Android with a clean interface.</p>

<h3>Features</h3>

<ul>
<li>Import configs from QR code, clipboard, files or subscriptions</li>
<li>Group configs by subscription and update every group at once</li>
<li>One-tap latency test for all configs</li>
<li>Flexible routing rules and per-app proxy</li>
<li>Light and dark themes</li>
</ul>
